# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project must be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
Examples of expected behavior include giving actionable feedback, respecting
different experiences, accepting correction gracefully, and keeping technical
disagreement focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, publication of another
person's private information, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, and other
spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainer through their GitHub
profile. For a concern that must remain confidential, use the repository's
private **Security → Report a vulnerability** channel and identify the report as
a code-of-conduct matter.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct is inconsistent with this
standard. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.
